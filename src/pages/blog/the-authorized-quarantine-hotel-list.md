The federal government's new regulations for travelers coming into Canada took effect on February 22, 2021 including a directive requiring passengers to spend around 3 nights in a quarantine hotel at their own expense until they receive their results of a mandatory COVID-19 test.

If travelers' test results come back negative, they are able to go home for the remainder of the 14-day mandatory isolation period. But, if they are found to be positive for the virus, they will be forced to quarantine in a designated government facility to prevent further spread.

Designated hotels are only located near the **four** airports in Canada that international travel has now been limited to since the beginning of the February 2021. They are **Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto, and Montreal.**
